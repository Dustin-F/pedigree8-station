PEDIGREE8 STATION — TABLET SETUP
================================

1. BEFORE INSTALLING (one time)
   - Settings > Update & Security > Activation: if it says "Windows 10 in S mode",
     switch out of S mode (free). S mode blocks this app.
   - Settings > Update & Security > Windows Update: install all updates, then restart.
   - Settings > System > Storage: make sure at least 1 GB is free.

2. INSTALL
   - Copy this whole folder from the USB stick to the tablet (e.g. to Downloads).
   - Double-click  install.bat
   - The app opens full screen with your spiders already in it.
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": tap "More info" > "Run anyway".

3. CAMERA (for scanning labels)
   - Settings > Privacy > Camera: turn ON "Allow apps to access your camera"
     and "Allow desktop apps to access your camera".

4. MAKE IT A STATION (recommended)
   - Start automatically without a password:
       press Windows+R, type  netplwiz  , press Enter, untick
       "Users must enter a user name and password", OK.
   - Screen and sleep: Settings > System > Power & sleep:
       "When plugged in, turn off after" = 10 or 15 minutes, "Sleep" = Never.
   - Windows Update restarts: Settings > Update & Security > Windows Update >
       Change active hours = the hours you use the station.

5. EVERYDAY USE
   - Leaving full screen: Backup & settings > Full screen: OFF  (or press F11 with a keyboard).
   - Closing the app:       Backup & settings > Close the app.
   - It restarts by itself when the tablet is switched on.

6. BACKUPS
   - A backup is made automatically every day; the last 14 stay on the tablet.
   - Once a week or so: plug in a USB stick > Backup & settings > Back up to USB.
     Keep that stick somewhere else — it is your copy if the tablet breaks.
   - Restore: Backup & settings > Restore a backup... (your current data is
     saved first, so a restore can itself be undone).

Your data lives in  C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\Pedigree8
To uninstall (data is kept): right-click uninstall.ps1 > Run with PowerShell.
